# Tilted-bed CNC router: build procedure

Generated by router.py; every step below is checked (R57-R68).

## Floor frame (welded where the machine stands)

### F01 Weld nuts and levelling feet on the floor rails

Weld an M16 nut under each rail at the five leg stations. Screw a levelling foot into each and set all ten to 30 mm under the rail. Stand the front and rear rails on their feet where the machine will live, front rail along the chalk line, 2 m clear in front for loading sheets.

- Heaviest handling: floor_frame.front_rail, 38.4 kg, carried by 2 (19.2 kg each)
- Tools: MIG welder, tape, chalk line, 24 mm spanner
- Parts: floor_frame.front_rail, floor_frame.rear_rail, floor_frame.foot_nut_0_0, floor_frame.foot_nut_0_1, floor_frame.foot_nut_1_0, floor_frame.foot_nut_1_1, floor_frame.foot_nut_2_0, floor_frame.foot_nut_2_1, floor_frame.foot_nut_3_0, floor_frame.foot_nut_3_1, floor_frame.foot_nut_4_0, floor_frame.foot_nut_4_1, feet.foot_0_0, feet.foot_0_1, feet.foot_1_0, feet.foot_1_1, feet.foot_2_0, feet.foot_2_1, feet.foot_3_0, feet.foot_3_1, feet.foot_4_0, feet.foot_4_1

### F02 Cross members and outriggers

Clamp the five cross members between the rails at the leg stations and tack them. Check the diagonals agree within 2 mm, then tack the five outriggers to the front rail's front face. Weld out all joints, alternating ends.

- Heaviest handling: floor_frame.outrigger_3, 3.2 kg, carried by 1 (3.2 kg each)
- Tools: MIG welder, framing square, tape, 4 F-clamps
- Parts: floor_frame.cross_0, floor_frame.cross_1, floor_frame.cross_2, floor_frame.cross_3, floor_frame.cross_4, floor_frame.outrigger_0, floor_frame.outrigger_1, floor_frame.outrigger_2, floor_frame.outrigger_3, floor_frame.outrigger_4

### F03 Level the floor frame

Level the frame on its feet to 0.5 mm/m along both rails and across, with a machinist's level on the rails. Lock the foot nuts.

- Tools: machinist's level 0.05 mm/m, 24 mm spanner

### F04 Rear posts

Cut a plywood template to the posts' angle from the drawing. Tack each post to the rear rail on its station with the template under it and brace its top to the front rail with a timber batten. Leave them tacked only: their tops are welded to the bed in B06.

- Heaviest handling: floor_frame.rear_post_0, 9.7 kg, carried by 1 (9.7 kg each)
- Tools: MIG welder, angle template, 5 timber braces
- Temporary: timber braces from each post top to the front rail, until B06
- Parts: floor_frame.rear_post_0, floor_frame.rear_post_1, floor_frame.rear_post_2, floor_frame.rear_post_3, floor_frame.rear_post_4

### F05 Cabinet stand

Weld the two stubs onto the front and rear rails' right ends (butt, ground flush), the cross member between their ends, the two uprights on the rear stub (plumb, 520 apart) and the two weld nuts; screw in the stand's two feet and level them with the frame.

## Bed (welded in place on the floor frame)

### B01 Bottom rail beam

Weld the end caps on the beam on trestles. Lift it onto the front rail with its back face 10 deg back from vertical (digital angle gauge), hold it with two timber props and G-clamps, and tack it to the front rail at each station.

- Heaviest handling: bed.rail_beam_bottom, 42.4 kg, carried by 2 (21.2 kg each)
- Weld bed.beam_cap_bottom_0 to bed.rail_beam_bottom: fillet all round, ground flush
- Weld bed.beam_cap_bottom_1 to bed.rail_beam_bottom: fillet all round, ground flush
- Tools: MIG welder, digital angle gauge, 2 trestles, 4 G-clamps
- Temporary: 2 timber props under the beam's front face, until B03
- Parts: bed.rail_beam_bottom, bed.beam_cap_bottom_0, bed.beam_cap_bottom_1

### B02 Top rail beam

Weld its end caps on trestles. Two people lift it onto the rear post tops, one at each end, standing on platform steps. Clamp it to the posts. Set the rail centres 1398 mm apart up the slope at both ends and in the middle, the beams parallel within 1 mm and the face 10 deg back; tack it to each post.

- Heaviest handling: bed.rail_beam_top, 42.4 kg, carried by 2 (21.2 kg each)
- Weld bed.beam_cap_top_0 to bed.rail_beam_top: fillet all round, ground flush

- Parts: bed.rail_beam_top, bed.beam_cap_top_0, bed.beam_cap_top_1

### B04 Rail lands, rack lands, X chain tray

Clamp each land flat bar to its beam on the layout lines (rail centres 1398 apart, racks 60 mm outboard). Stitch weld 30 mm every 150 mm, alternating sides and working from the middle out. Weld the X chain tray, with its rear shelf for the trunking, on the top beam's top face.

- Heaviest handling: bed.x_chain_tray, 7.6 kg, carried by 1 (7.6 kg each)
- Weld bed.rail_land_bottom to bed.rail_beam_bottom: 30 mm stitches at 150, both sides, alternating

- Parts: bed.rail_land_bottom, bed.rail_land_top, bed.rack_land_bottom, bed.rack_land_top, bed.x_chain_tray

### B05 Flatten the lands, drill and tap

With a 1 m precision straightedge and feeler gauges, grind and file the rail lands until each is straight within 0.05 mm per metre and the pair are coplanar within 0.05 mm (a straightedge across both, at 300 mm steps, with a level on it). Drill and tap the M6 holes with a drill guide from the rail and rack hole patterns.

- Tools: 1 m straightedge (DIN 874/1), feeler gauges, flap disc, mill file, drill guide, M6 taps, 5.0 drill

### B06 Weld the bed to the floor frame

Weld the bottom beam to the front rail along both sides of the contact line, and the rear posts' tops to the top beam's back. Remove the braces. Re-check the bed's lean (10 deg) and the floor frame's level.

- Tools: MIG welder, digital angle gauge

### B07 Clean and paint

Wire-brush the welds, prime and paint the frame. Mask the lands and their tapped holes.

- Tools: wire brush, primer, masking tape

### B08 Substrate boards

Lay the three 25 mm MDF boards on the cross members, each end on the middle of a cross member, PVA glue on the members, and screw through into each member.

- Heaviest handling: bed.substrate.0, 29.0 kg, carried by 2 (14.5 kg each)
- 42 x 4.8 x 45 self-drilling wafer-head screw 10-16: cordless driver, PH2 / 8 mm hex bit
- Tools: cordless drill, PVA glue
- Parts: bed.substrate.0, bed.substrate.1, bed.substrate.2

## Gantry column (welded on the bench, stood up onto the X blocks)
